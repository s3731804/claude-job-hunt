# Job queue

Mode: A. Worth applying
Current: none yet. "next" starts at A1.

One rolling queue. "search" adds new listings to the bottom of each tier and leaves Mode and Current alone. "next" works through the current tier until it's empty, then moves on to the next tier (A → B → C → back to A). The Decision column is filled in as we go: Built, Passed, Skipped, Closed or Stale. Every listing ID seen is in `seen-jobs.tsv`.
